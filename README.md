# The Legend of Zelda: Gulls Of Life Randomizer

## What is Gulls of Life?
Gulls of Life is a romhack based on Oracle of Ages which is a sequel to Link’s Awakening that takes place after the Wind Fish wakes up. 

## Where it all started
Marin wishes that she could come back as a seagull and as a result her wish comes true and ends up coming back as a seagull after all, on a side where no one dreams anymore.

## Where you would normally start in the game:
You would start in the remains of Koholint Island where you are greeted by Marin as a seagull (the randomizer will skip this sequence, along with the cutscenes).

## The Goal
You are supposed to restore everyone’s dreams to make them all come true by curing the dark from all 4 dungeons (one is currently functioning in the release, 2 in the dev version)

## Why this randomizer
The game can get boring if you beat the same story many times. A purpose of this randomizer is to take all of the items from each location and shuffle them around, giving you a new experience time.
